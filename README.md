# CI Career Phenotype Coach

**Live page: https://jgenk.github.io/ci-phenotype-coach/**

A free, self-paced planning tool for clinicians (physicians, nurses, advanced practice providers, pharmacists, and others) who work in, or are curious about, clinical informatics. It walks you through four steps:

1. Where you are now
2. Your current phenotype: rate 20 kinds of informatics work to see which common patterns of work yours resembles
3. Your goal phenotype: the work you want in 3–5 years
4. Gaps and a plan of 2–3 next steps

You finish with a one-page report. You can use any AI tool for any step, or none.

**This is a prototype.** The phenotypes are a vocabulary for reflection and mentoring conversations. They are not a validated classification of people, and the weights come from a survey of physicians. Expect changes.

**Privacy:** the page runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are saved only in this browser (localStorage) and in any file you choose to save. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, with one exception: the AMIA 2026 workshop copy (`/amia-2026/`) offers two short optional, anonymous surveys, one in step 1 and one at the wrap-up. Only if you agree and submit one, it sends your survey answers plus your step 1 menu choices about your role and workplace, how you're using the coach, and which AI tool. No name, email, specialty, or anything you type in the steps. If you use an AI tool, you copy the prompt into it yourself, so that tool's privacy settings apply. Don't enter patient information or confidential details.
